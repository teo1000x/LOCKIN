# Business Verification prep: Canu Group, LLC (Wyoming)

_Written 19 August 2026. Companion to `docs/23-META-FILING-CHECKLIST.md` step 3. This is the
"what to have ready" list that removes every known rejection cause before the founder clicks
submit. Verification checks the LEGAL ENTITY, not the brand: the portfolio is verified as the
LLC, and Bonded Crew is the product name inside it. No new entity is needed._

**Note on spelling: the founder said the LLC name aloud and it may be "Canu" or "Canoe".
The only correct spelling is the one on the Wyoming filing. Copy it from the Articles of
Organization character for character everywhere it appears, including this doc's title once
confirmed.**

## Founder checklist, in order

1. **Confirm the LLC is Active.** Wyoming requires a $60 annual report yearly with a 60-day
   grace period; after that the LLC goes delinquent, then administratively dissolved.
   An idle LLC is exactly the kind that lapses. Check the status on the Wyoming Secretary of
   State business search (wyobiz.wyo.gov). Delinquent: file the annual report online
   immediately. Dissolved: reinstatement is $100 plus back fees, and the timeline changes,
   so surface it right away.
2. **EIN.** If none exists, apply free at irs.gov (never a paid third-party site); the CP-575
   confirmation letter arrives instantly as a PDF and is the strongest single verification
   document. If the letter is lost, call the IRS for a replacement 147C. Articles of
   Organization alone can carry verification, but having both doubles the document options.
3. **Documents as PDFs.** Articles of Organization plus the EIN letter (CP-575 or 147C).
   High-resolution, uncropped, full-page scans. State and IRS issued documents only;
   self-prepared tax filings are not accepted.
4. **Exact legal name.** The number one rejection cause is a name mismatch. Copy the name
   from the Articles into the Business Portfolio, including punctuation and the LLC suffix.
   Never "Bonded Crew" in the legal name field.
5. **Address matches the documents.** Remotely formed Wyoming LLCs usually show the
   registered agent's address on the Articles; if so, use that address in the Business
   Manager profile. Using a different address requires a document placing the LLC there
   (bank statement or utility bill in the LLC's name). Simplest: match the formation docs.
6. **Domain, site, and email.** bondedcrew.com purchased, hello@bondedcrew.com created and
   set as the business email (matching-domain email is identity evidence; personal gmail is
   not). Operator task once the domain is live: add "Bonded Crew is a product of
   [exact legal name]" to the site footer and the terms page so a reviewer visiting the site
   sees the entity named.
7. **Personal Facebook account with two-factor enabled.** Portfolios hang off a personal
   login and Meta requires 2FA for admins of verified businesses. An aged, real account in
   good standing helps.
8. **A phone that can receive the verification call or text.**

## The submit-state test

Wyoming SOS says Active. Portfolio legal name, address, and documents all say the same thing
character for character. Business email is at the domain and confirmed. Website is live and
names the entity. Documents are legible full-page PDFs.

## If it still bounces

Business Verification rejections are recoverable: read the stated reason, fix that one field
or document, resubmit. The queue restarts in days. This is unlike App Review, where a sloppy
submission poisons later attempts, so there is no reason to submit verification half-ready.
